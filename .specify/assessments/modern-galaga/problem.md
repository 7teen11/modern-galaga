# Problem Definition: Lightweight, Shared Fun for Employees (behind the "modern Galaga" request)

- **Slug**: modern-galaga
- **Created**: 2026-11-04
- **Inputs used**: intake.md, research.md

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The idea arrived **as a solution** ("build a modern Galaga-style shooter with upgrades and a leaderboard"). Neither intake nor research names the underlying problem, so the statement below is **reverse-engineered and provisional**. `research.md` found **no internal demand signal** (confidence: high that the signal is missing). Every user, goal, and metric here is a hypothesis to confirm with the sponsor.

## Problem Statement

Employees may lack a low-effort, shared, and playful way to connect and compete casually with colleagues, especially across teams or remote/hybrid setups. Without one, informal social bonding and lighthearted company culture depend on ad-hoc events. - **Users**: Employees across the company. They would experience the gap as few casual, shared moments of fun and friendly competition with colleagues. Research suggests only a minority (~10%) are mainly motivated by competition/rankings, and most by social connection or mastery (research.md → Users & Demand, medium confidence). - **Stakeholders**:

  - HR / People & Culture: cares about engagement, inclusiveness, and avoiding demotivating public rankings (research.md → Evidence Against). [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: involved?]
  - IT / Security / Data Privacy: affected if employee identities or data are involved (research.md → Data & Constraints). [NEEDS CLARIFICATION]

## Goals

- Employees have a quick, enjoyable shared experience they take part in voluntarily. [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: confirm with sponsor]
- The experience produces social connection or friendly rivalry *across* teams/locations, not just within existing cliques. [NEEDS CLARIFICATION]
- Recognition through rankings feels fair and trustworthy to participants, so it doesn't cause disputes or resentment (research.md → leaderboard integrity, gamification pitfalls).
- Participation lasts beyond the novelty window, or meets a defined time-boxed purpose such as a specific event (research.md → novelty decay). [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: ongoing vs. event-bound]
- The outcome creates no IP/trademark exposure for the company (research.md → Data & Constraints).

## Non-Goals

- Not a commercial or public product. The audience is internal only (intake.md).
- Not a tool to measure, evaluate, or rank employees' work performance. Rankings, if any, concern play only.
- Not a reproduction of the original Galaga brand, name, art, or audio (research.md → trademark/copyright).
- Not a replacement for broader engagement programs, team-building, or HR initiatives.
- Not tied to monetary compensation or performance reviews. [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: whether non-monetary prizes are in scope]

## Success Metrics

- Share of employees who take part at least once in the first N weeks (baseline: unknown; target: [NEEDS CLARIFICATION]).

## Cost of Inaction

With no demonstrated demand or measured problem (research.md → Evidence Against), doing nothing appears to cost **little or nothing measurable**. Employees keep using existing casual options and ad-hoc events. The main potential losses are a missed culture/engagement opportunity and, if this was a learning or hackathon initiative, a missed learning or showcase opportunity. Both are unquantified.
